## What the research changed from the usual story

- **First sale.** Mead's trademark filings claim first use on October 30, 1978. The remembered date of the Wichita test is August 1978.
- **The 1981 national launch.** The Trapper Keeper was already in Utah and California stores in 1979, labelled "NEW from Mead." The 1981 date is best read as the start of the national TV campaign.
- **Snap to Velcro.** Store ads date the switch to July–August 1982. A "Designer Trapper Keeper" was on sale by 1985, three years before the "Designer Series" date of 1988.
- **The "Organizer."** Westab's binder of 1973 already had pockets, a clip and pad inside the back cover, and assorted cover designs.
- **School bans.** The only period document found is a 1994 *Salt Lake Tribune* column saying a school's list excluded Trapper Keepers, with no reason given. Velcro noise and size as the reasons come only from 2013 and 2022 accounts.
- **Credit.** The patents name Wyant and Seaborn for the parts. Crutchfield's sole credit comes from his own 2013 telling.
