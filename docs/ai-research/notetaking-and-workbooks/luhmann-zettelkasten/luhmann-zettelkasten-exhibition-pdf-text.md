An identi

Contemporary Zettelkasten teaching o

A manageable experiment with links
Over one week, make twelve paper slips or Markdown 
iles 
or a current project. Give each a
stable identi

its source and locator where applicable. Include two unresolved or contradictory entries. As
new material arrives, put it beside a related entry or link it to one; write a sentence
explaining the relation. Make a keyword list with three entrances. These numbers are a
classroom constraint, not Luhmann’s rules.
Leave the collection 
or two days. Return through one keyword and

Produce a 150-word argument or a one-minute rehearsal proposal. Log one surprise, one
dead end, and the time spent retrieving material. Then add a thirteenth note that challenges
the route’s strongest claim. Success means being able to explain a change in the project’s
question, including a use

Run a second pass with AI, asking it to suggest three links between named entries and

vocabulary. A

di

prematurely compatible.
The experiment tests a possibility rather than reproducing decades o
