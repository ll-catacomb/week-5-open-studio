# Six visual candidates for the wall

These are identifiable sources, not six cleared reproduction files. The essay embeds one low-resolution research preview. Obtain item-level reproduction terms before printing archival images; online access does not establish a reuse license. Pair an archival object with a short explanation of what it can and cannot prove.

| No. | Candidate and source | Proposed caption | Access and reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The three-part *Couragemodell 1949*, Henschel, **1958**: text, photographs, notes. [Edition record](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL2824306M/Couragemodell_1949); [Jones’s description](https://www.jstor.org/stable/10.1525/j.ctt7zw3wm), chapter 6. | **One production, three kinds of record.** The title’s 1949 dates the model production; this published package appeared in 1958. Its script, photographs, and commentary perform different jobs. | Obtain a library copy or archive-approved image of the package or a spread. No open image license established. A student-made diagram of the three volumes is an available alternative, labeled as a reconstruction. |
| 2 | Hainer Hill’s scene-three sequence, including the silent scream, reproduced in [*Brecht on Performance*](https://www.bloomsbury.com/au/brecht-on-performance-9781350077065/), 2018, pp. 226–227; equivalent 2015 pages 211–212. [Publisher preview and picture credits](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781472515438_A24067716/preview-9781472515438_A24067716.pdf), printed p. ix. | **An action made available for study.** Weigel’s posture survives through a selected photographic sequence. The image is evidence of performance, not independent proof of the historical photograph that may have informed it. | Picture credits assign these images to Hainer Hill, © Akademie der Künste. Request an approved scan and exact item date from the Brecht Archive. Do not label every image in the model as Berlau’s, or assume this gesture was present on opening night. |
| 3 | Brecht and Helene Weigel rehearsing *Mother Courage*, **1951**, photographed by Hainer Hill; displayed on the [Akademie’s production-history page](https://frontend.adk.de/news/2020/05-11/die-couragefigur-hellis-jetzt-herrlich-von-grosser-kuhnheit). | **The model continued to change.** This photograph is dated 1951, two years after the foundational Berlin opening. Keep successive rehearsal histories visible instead of treating the model as one frozen event. | Source page explicitly credits © Akademie der Künste, Berlin, photograph Hainer Hill. The image is identified on the public page, but its full-resolution file was not retrieved here. Request reproduction and item identifier from the archive. |
| 4 | Helen Warner’s *London Road* **2011** production photograph, with crossing police tape, in the [National Theatre finding aid](https://images.nationaltheatre.org.uk/uploads/2025/02/Teaching-Practitioners-Using-the-Archive_2025.pdf#page=7), printed p. 7. Related series: RNT/PR/3/940. | **A staged space, not its origin story.** London Road, National Theatre, 2011. Photograph: Helen Warner. This view records the result; the evidence consulted does not identify the source behind this arrangement. | A cropped research preview is [included locally](assets/london-road-2011-research-preview.png). Crop removes surrounding text and retains the photograph; credit accompanies it. Public PDF supplies photographer credit, not an open reuse license. Request wall/exhibition permission and a higher-resolution file from the NT Archive. |
| 5 | A page from *London Road* prompt scripts, **2011**, RNT/SM/1/643a–c, located through the [production catalogue](https://catalogue.nationaltheatre.org.uk/CalmView/Record.aspx?id=PERF7724&src=CalmView.Performance). | **Knowledge for running a performance.** A prompt script turns agreed choices into information that others can coordinate and repeat. Read it beside, rather than as a substitute for, exploratory research. | Candidate only: the original pages were not inspected. Request a page that demonstrates cues and confirm its maker, annotations, date, and copy-specific function before finalizing the caption. No open reproduction terms found. Do not invent a facsimile of unexamined cues. |
| 6 | The **9 and 10 March 2011** research corrections concerning Bailey the dog, publicly reproduced in the [NT finding aid](https://images.nationaltheatre.org.uk/uploads/2025/02/Teaching-Practitioners-Using-the-Archive_2025.pdf#page=10), printed p. 10. | **A working resource can correct itself.** A rehearsal sound request is followed by amended research. The change matters because somebody records and communicates it. | Use an original two-date graphic with short paraphrases, clearly labeled “reconstruction from published report extracts,” or ask for permission to reproduce original reports. The public extracts do not name the individual note-taker. They do not prove which sound effect ultimately played. |

Suggested hanging order: 1–3 show documentation becoming a future resource; 4–6 show source material entering a current production. Place the [original process diagram](assets/source-to-production.svg) between them. Leave the uncertain historical source photograph as a written question rather than selecting an evocative but unverified war photograph.
