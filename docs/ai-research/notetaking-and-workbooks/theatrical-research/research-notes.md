# Research scope and archival access

Research conducted 2026-10-05 for the week 5 theme-inspirations folder. This is the process record, separate from the exhibition prose.

## Consultation and source limits

Gideon Lester’s [A.R.T. article](https://americanrepertorytheater.org/media/a-model-of-courage/) was read in full. The [1958 edition record](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL2824306M/Couragemodell_1949) and publicly visible introduction to chapter 6 of [David Richard Jones’s book](https://www.jstor.org/stable/10.1525/j.ctt7zw3wm) were consulted; the complete JSTOR chapter was not.

For *Brecht on Performance*, the [publisher’s edition information](https://www.bloomsbury.com/au/brecht-on-performance-9781350077065/) and [2015 preview](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781472515438_A24067716/preview-9781472515438_A24067716.pdf) verify contents and picture credits. The preview does **not** contain the Courage chapter. Passage wording and 2018 pagination were inspected in a [searchable third-party transcription](https://dokumen.pub/brecht-on-performance-messingkauf-and-modelbooks-9781350077065-9781350077096-9781350077089.html). The scholarly edition is the cited work. Check a licensed library copy before using verbatim quotations or ordering specific page scans. The essay paraphrases the passages.

Martina Kolb’s article was read through the [University of Wisconsin digitization](https://digital.library.wisc.edu/1711.dl/M3HLL3GNJRCAF8S), with relevant text and endnotes extracted from its [PDF](https://asset.library.wisc.edu/1711.dl/B47LC3AJDTMVG8H/E/file-3c1ab.pdf). It is in *Communications* 34, **June 2005**, pp. 80–93; a search index’s recent date is not its publication date. Notes 13–15 point to the model and to *Unerbittlich das Richtige zeigend: Helene Weigel 1900–1971* (Akademie der Künste, 2000), p. 36. The underlying exhibition volume was not separately inspected.

The Akademie’s [Brecht Archive description](https://adk.de/archiv-der-kuenste/abteilungen-fachbereiche/bertolt-brecht-archiv) and [digitization project](https://frontend.adk.de/archiv/2017/digitalisierung-der-modellbucher-und-des-hainer-hill-fotoarchivs-im-bertolt-brecht-archiv) identify holdings. The [1951 photograph page](https://frontend.adk.de/news/2020/05-11/die-couragefigur-hellis-jetzt-herrlich-von-grosser-kuhnheit) was available as indexed archive text, but live image-page fetches failed. Its past temporary streaming announcement does not prove that the recording streams today.

For London Road, the [production catalogue](https://catalogue.nationaltheatre.org.uk/CalmView/Record.aspx?id=PERF7724&src=CalmView.Performance) and [National Theatre finding aid](https://images.nationaltheatre.org.uk/uploads/2025/02/Teaching-Practitioners-Using-the-Archive_2025.pdf), especially pp. 6–10, were consulted. Susie Ferguson wrote the guide in **2024**; it is hosted in a 2025 directory. The PDF was downloaded temporarily, and the production-photo page was rendered and visually inspected. The guide is a later educational resource reproducing selected historical records, not the original rehearsal dossier. Some individual catalogue entries failed to load; the parent record supplies their references.

Published maker interviews were read: [Blythe, 2012](https://www.whatsonstage.com/news/brief-encounter-with-london-roads-alecky-blythe_3237/), [Cork with Michael Friedman, 2016](https://extendedplay.thecivilians.org/composers-michael-friedman-and-adam-cork-discuss-the-verbatim-film-musical-london-road-91516/), and [Cork, 2017](https://theoxfordculturereview.com/2017/01/29/the-magic-wand-of-artistic-transformation-adam-cork-on-london-road/). They are retrospective testimony. Evi Stamatiou’s [repository manuscript](https://eprints.chi.ac.uk/4579/1/A%20Brechtian%20perspective%20on%20London%20Road.pdf) was read in relevant sections; its header calls publication forthcoming, so no unverified final publication date is supplied.
