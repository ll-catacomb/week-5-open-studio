# Mental Floss as a route to sources

The article is two layers. The body is the 2013 story "through the eyes of Crutchfield," told from a 2013 interview with E. Bryant Crutchfield, then Mead's director of New Ventures. The June 2024 editor's note, written after Jon Wyant contested Crutchfield's account following the 2022 obituaries, reframes it and adds the patents and a 1976 memo. Almost everything in the body is retrospective memory, given 35 years after the events by someone with a stake in the credit.
