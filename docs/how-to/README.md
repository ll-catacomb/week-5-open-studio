# How-to

Ask Claude how to make your project, and keep its ideas here: one Markdown file per plan, technique, or practice exercise (for example `how-to/shoot-overhead-cooking.md`). These are suggestions to test in your studies, not instructions to follow. Check them against your readings and your examples, and write down in your [Notebook](/notebook) what worked when you tried them.
