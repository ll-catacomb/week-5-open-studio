Original reports elsewhere in theme inspirations were retained.

Review read the main arguments, cases, transformation chains, source leads, and access records of all ten reports. The coordinator also independently checked representative source claims, including Plutarch's opening, Gu Yanwu's procedural preface, and Franssen's discussion of al-Ṣafadī's searches. An authorship/date inconsistency in report 09 was corrected by its author. Final verification checked the ten numbered outputs, frontmatter, and local Markdown links.

This is a sourced research collection and a program for further work, not a complete archival collation. Many sources were read as extracted text or through scholarly editions; some manuscript images, intermediate drafts, recordings, and paywalled works remain uninspected. The individual reports identify those limits. Classroom demonstrations are labeled as modern reconstructions where the historical chain is incomplete. No claim of historical influence is inferred merely from a resemblance between systems.

All files remain local, uncommitted changes in the week repo. No publishing, Slack messages, Supabase queries, or changes to Marlon's personal notes were involved.
